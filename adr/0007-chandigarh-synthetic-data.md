# ADR 0007: Chandigarh Phase 1 data is synthetic

Status: accepted.

The initial Chandigarh demo may use licensed OpenStreetMap horizontal features, official administrative boundaries and clearly labelled synthetic facilities and restrictions. A live query found virtually no useful building-height coverage in OSM, so vertical obstacle geometry is explicitly synthetic and requires survey.

No map layer may be promoted from synthetic to authoritative without source, date, licence and safety-review metadata.
